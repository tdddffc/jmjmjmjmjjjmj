# Home-screen widgets

Add: long-press the home screen -> Widgets -> **Weather** (full) or **Weather alerts** (alerts only).
Both refresh every 30 minutes, when you open the app, and when you tap the 🔄 button on the widget.
Tapping anywhere else opens the app.

## Weather widget (resizable)
The widget adapts to its size:

| Height | Shows |
|---|---|
| small | location, big emoji, temperature, feels-like, high/low |
| ~130dp+ | + alert banner (first active alert, "+N more") |
| ~210dp+ | + hourly strip (4/6/8 hours) |
| ~250dp+ | + sunrise / daylight / sunset |
| ~300dp+ | + info tiles (4, or 8 from ~420dp) |
| ~360dp+ | + next days (3, then 5 from ~440dp, 7 from ~520dp) |
| ~560dp+ (full screen) | + complete alert list |

Stretch it to full screen height in your launcher to see everything.

## Settings -> Home-screen widgets
- **Look:** Weather colours / Dark / Light.
- **Colour follows the weather in the next:** Now, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours. The widget takes the colour of the worst weather in that window (thunder > snow > rain > fog > cloud > clear; clear nights turn deep purple).
- **Sections:** turn the alert banner, hourly strip, info tiles, days and sunrise/sunset on or off.
- **Hours in the strip** 4/6/8 and **days in the list** 3/5/7.
- **Info tiles:** pick up to 8 (wind, gusts, humidity, UV, snow today, rain today, rain chance, pressure, feels like, visibility) in the order you want.
- **Sunrise & sunset icons:** Classic 🌅🌇, Mountain 🌄🌆, Sun & moon ☀️🌙, Rooster & owl 🐓🦉. Applies to the widget and the app.

## Alerts widget
Lists your active alerts (black ice, snow, wind...) from Custom Weather Alerts, or "No alerts". Uses the Dark or Light look.

Note: Android widgets cannot play animations, so widget emoji are still.
